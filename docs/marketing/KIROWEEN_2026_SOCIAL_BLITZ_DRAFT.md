#KiroweenHackathon26

A rejected access card. A door held open. Two systems speaking different languages. What if the building could finally tell the whole story?

We’re building **GhostBridge**, an open-source, local-first security intelligence experience by **InnerChispa LLC** for #Kiroween2026.

Instead of another dashboard full of disconnected alarms, GhostBridge turns synthetic legacy relay and access-controller signals into one explainable incident, with original timestamps, evidence hashes and an interactive 2.5D reconstruction.

**What Kiro changed for us:** spec-driven development made the difference between asking an agent to “build a feature” and defining exactly what the software must prove. Kiro IDE helped build our severity filtering, two legacy adapters, Python/JavaScript case correlation and interactive evidence reconstruction across four reviewed pull requests.

An early held-open door is excluded because it happened before the denied access. A later 130-second open door, 270 seconds after the rejection, qualifies. The important part is not just that the case appears; it's that we can explain **why**.

Our next step is to make the experience even more interactive, so a visitor can change the inputs and test the rules instead of only watching one scripted replay.
