# InnerChispa | Visual Product & Sales Playbook
**Version:** 2026-11-10 | **Scope:** GhostBridge now; reusable across InnerOS, PC Doctor, Physical Guardian, CreatorCore and future offers.

## Business-first principle

Every public product experience and film should sell a believable desired outcome before explaining its technology. Start from a customer's urgent problem, show the emotionally clear before/after, and then support the promise with actual proof and a roadmap. The user approves showcasing **future product vision** visually and cinematically; do not turn every scene into a compliance disclaimer. Distinguish shipping/current screens from future UX with one clean chapter title, e.g. **PRODUCT VISION**, and put detailed status in the technical description.

**Do not let a developer tool become the product.** Kiro is a build accelerator (specs, steering, hooks, MCP, Powers, agents, CLI/Web/IDE/Crew). GitHub-hosted source, open APIs and local inference remain the durable assets. For Kiroween only, authentic Kiro use is a judged requirement, and the existing four Kiro PRs are part of the proven story.

## Visual quality bar for every InnerChispa product

1. **The customer sees the result in 10 seconds.** Hero experience = a tangible property, real workflow, result, and one clear action, not technology logos or engineering jargon.
2. **Looks like a product worth buying.** Premium typography, restrained gradients/light, intentional color and motion, polished states and responsive geometry. No UI made from uniform cards stacked endlessly.
3. **Directly interactive.** Guests change relevant inputs and see actual computed results. No mock click paths that pretend to calculate.
4. **Contextual visualization.** Map, floorplan, exploded equipment, timeline, incident storyboard, operator workflow or 3D scene, chosen according to the product. Spatial context should explain—not conceal—the evidence.
5. **Scenario transitions sell outcomes.** Show “fragmented signals → explained case → authorized decision.” Do not overwhelm judges with raw hashes before showing why the case matters.
6. **Evidence on demand.** Engineering rigor remains one deliberate click away: case IDs, original timestamps, hashes, event-source provenance, audit export, limitations.
7. **Premium cinematic motion.** UI motion communicates causality. Use depth, transitions, environment and informed camera movement, with reduced-motion fallback.
8. **Reusable brand language.** Dark charcoal/navy, electric lime evidence validation, deep teal flow, amber warning, lavender source provenance. Product-specific identities may adapt this palette; preserve consistent typographic hierarchy.
9. **Conversion architecture.** After the wow moment, explain who buys, why, and the next action (contact/schedule site audit/pilot); don't require filling a form to try the public demo.
10. **Measure on real devices.** Chrome/Edge, 390px mobile, keyboard and reduced motion; no overloaded JS, 404s or unreadable metrics.

## GhostBridge | Signature experience: “GhostBridge Control Room”

**User promise:** "See the whole building story, not a pile of alarms."

### Layer A — Fast first impression (5–15 seconds)
- Full-bleed cinematic architectural diagram or 2.5D/3D stylized high-rise entry, night lighting.
- One door/reader/camera source becomes active, revealing a trace in the event timeline.
- One CTA: **Investigate an incident** / **Build your own incident**.
- Keep the original deterministic demo visible and working for reviewer confidence.

### Layer B — Interactive investigation (60 seconds)
- **Signal Forge** (draft PR #10, authored directly by ChatGPT): user adjusts denied-access-to-door interval, door-open duration and optional camera-offline source; the actual verified ledger and correlator decide.
- Preset **299s and 120s = correlate** vs **301s and 119s = do not correlate**. Display two paths side-by-side as a clear before/after comparison where performance permits.
- **Evidence Reconstruction** (genuine Kiro PR #8): play/step the event history inside the 2.5D scene, highlight exclusions and exact evidence references.
- Add a visually clear outcome panel: "Case created / no case", why, risk priority, and proposed operator next step. Suggested next steps are recommendations, not automatic device actuation.
- A “Show engineering proof” affordance reveals SHA-256 hashes and source rules.

### Layer C — Aspirational enterprise version (film/storytelling)
- Future mobile incident notifications with operator acknowledgement and escalation.
- A digital twin showing door, camera and source overlays by floor/zone.
- A multi-building console and productized local appliance.
- Existing property infrastructure modernization without replacing everything.
- These screens may be cinematic **product-vision compositions** long before all integrations are released, with a single clear transition.

## Commercial product positioning

- **Offer:** “GhostBridge by InnerChispa: Local Evidence Intelligence for Existing Buildings.”
- **Ideal customer:** private residential developments, building managers, facility security contractors, industrial properties with mixed-age equipment.
- **Primary initial purchase path:** site risk/infrastructure assessment → paid isolated pilot with real or synthetic sources as agreed → supported appliance rollout with integration/maintenance agreement.
- **Trust differentiator:** privacy-preserving local processing, explanation per signal, evidence provenance and modular connectors; not just another camera dashboard.
- **Revenue potential:** recurring support and operations contract after installation, site-specific quotes. Do not publish unapproved blanket pricing.
- **Product relationship:** GhostBridge can be the approachable commercial experience, and the InnerOS Physical Guardian / Vigilos integration becomes an optional deeper production offering.

## Premium video standard (applies to every product)

**Narrative:** *problem → consequence → unique mechanism → real proof → future product experience → buyer outcome → brand*. No mandatory talking-head intro.

**Target length:** 2:45–2:55 for the Kiroween film; shorter 15s/30s/60s cutdowns for social and sales.

**Shot composition:**
1. 0:00–0:15: atmospheric real-looking building scene, rejected badge + open door.
2. 0:15–0:35: isolated signals unify in spatial composition.
3. 0:35–1:20: actual running GhostBridge and Signal Forge behavior, with crisp cursor focus and real event-linked result.
4. 1:20–1:48: Kiro-authored Evidence Reconstruction in controlled visual sequence.
5. 1:48–2:20: **PRODUCT VISION** transition: mobile response, command center, digital twin, multi-property business impact.
6. 2:20–2:42: 3–4 authentic glimpses of Kiro IDE, spec artifacts, merged PRs, verified CI.
7. 2:42–2:52: business result + one memorable promise + InnerChispa/GhostBridge branding.

**Production rules:** Owner may appear for 3–8 seconds to establish human leadership; the rest is voiceover/scene and product. Voice in natural US English, closed captions, professional original/licensed sound design, color grade, fluid transitions, and a product-vision sequence that demonstrates intended future operation. Reuse previous V1/V2 assets; create the final costly render only after actual frontend captures are approved. Prioritize local rendering/Blender/FFmpeg/DaVinci, and external AI video/voice only if local quality is insufficient and spend is authorized.

**Judging compliance:** Official 2026 Kiroween rules require the application to run consistently and function as depicted in video and text; the film can still show *explicitly described future vision* without presenting those future screens as already live. Avoid repeated disclaimers or narration that undermines the sale. In Devpost, use crisp “Available now” and “Next” sections.

## Quality gates before calling the project ready

- Public custom domain is reachable without credentials.
- Signal Forge and Evidence Reconstruction are responsive, truly calculated and use original source hashes.
- End-to-end judge path completes comfortably within two minutes on desktop and mobile.
- Screenshots and test artifacts for the *current* build are available and inspected.
- One video speaks convincingly to real property operators, with memorable product scenes.
- Devpost technical Kiro provenance is accurate and all required submission fields/video are actually submitted.

**Owner decision:** We are creating sellable products, not showing off tool usage. The front-end and cinematic sales experience must feel like the future product, while GitHub and the local-first architecture keep ownership independent of any IDE vendor.
